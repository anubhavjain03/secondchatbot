What moves the forecast

    How this is collected and calculated

    A scheduled run collects public prices once a day: AWS price files and the Capacity Blocks page, the Azure Retail Prices API, Oracle's price list API, Vast.ai marketplace offers, the getdeploying.com comparison pages (dozens of neoclouds, marketplaces and serverless platforms), and Silicon Data's public index pages.

    Prices are per GPU-hour in USD. Each row keeps the provider's cheapest SKU per region, term and size. Rows below a quarter of the median are dropped as fractional or mislabeled listings. Summary figures count each provider once, at its cheapest offer. GB200 and GB300 (Grace) systems are grouped under B200 and B300 and labeled in the variant column.

    Forecast: each month the log price moves by an analog depreciation rate (the H100's path after launch) plus a momentum term from recent index moves, which halves every three months. Once 30 days of this tracker's own history exist, half the momentum comes from it. The range widens with the square root of the horizon. These are scenarios, not predictions.

    Private estimate: the 35th percentile of providers' published reserved rates (6 months or longer) plus index and commitment evidence. The range runs from the lowest credible rate to the 25th percentile of on-demand prices.
